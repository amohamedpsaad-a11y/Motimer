What it does

Everything the timer in your head forgets to track.

Motimer runs alongside TryRating.com and turns every task into a data point automatically, without extra clicks.

🎯

Automatic task detection

Captures the Task ID and Request ID straight from the page the moment a task opens nothing to copy by hand.

⏱️

Live floating timer

A small, unobtrusive clock sits on the page itself, showing elapsed time next to your Estimated Rating Time.

🔔

ERT alerts

A sound and on-screen warning when a task runs long, so you notice before it eats your average.

📊

Daily dashboard

Tasks grouped by type, with count, average time, and ERT comparisons for the day no spreadsheet required.

📜

Searchable activity log

Every submitted task recorded with its type, time spent, and ERT editable and easy to review later.

☁️

Optional Drive backup

Sign in with Google to back up your log to your own private Drive space visible only to you, never to us.

How it works

Three steps, then it disappears into the background.

01 · Install

Add Motimer to Chrome

One click from the Chrome Web Store, then connect your Google account to activate Motimer.

02 · Open a task

Motimer clocks in automatically

The moment a rating task loads on TryRating.com, the timer starts no button to remember.

03 · Review your day

Check the dashboard whenever you like

See totals, averages, and ERT comparisons for every task type you worked that day.

Your task data stays on your google drive.

Motimer doesn't run a backend. There's no company database collecting what you rate, how long it takes, or who you are.
